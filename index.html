FAQ

Is this legally-binding e-sign?

No — not in v1. QuotePilot v1 is proposal draft + shareable link + PDF. For a binding signature, use PandaDoc / DocuSign / HelloSign, or have the client reply "Accepted" by email. E-sign with audit trail is on the roadmap and will be clearly labeled when it ships.

Where is my data stored?

In your browser only (localStorage) in v1. Nothing is sent to a server. Clear your browser data and you lose your pipeline — export CSV before you do.

Why 3 tiers?

Single-number proposals anchor low. Three tiers let clients choose value, not haggle price. Most pick Standard when it is clearly the best value.

What if the client has no budget?

Score them Red, send the polite decline, and move on. You just saved 6 hours.

Can I use this for my own business?

Yes. That is the point. Dogfood it on your next 5 leads and see if your close rate and average deal size go up.
